Cultural Heritage

The Royal Game of Kings & Courtyards

Dating back over 1,600 years, Pachisi is revered as the National Game of India. In the 16th century, the Mughal Emperor Akbar constructed a colossal stone Pachisi board in the courtyard of Fatehpur Sikri, utilizing sixteen harem maidens as living pawns moving at his command.
