Player Rights & Data Ethics

Privacy Policy

Mantrika Games is built on principles of cultural stewardship, thoughtful design, and player trust. We believe tabletop games should be tranquil sanctuaries—free from intrusive behavioral surveillance, hidden tracking, or sold data.

✦ Last Revised: October 2026 • Effective Worldwide
🛡️

No Behavioral Ads

We do not sell personal information or profile users for targeted advertising. Our games respect your screen and your privacy.

🎲

Local Computation

All 3D board physics, AI algorithms, and single-player games execute locally in your web browser or device storage without cloud dependencies.

🔒

Complete Deletion Rights

Players and private beta testers have full, irrevocable rights to request complete deletion of any stored data at any time.

This Privacy Policy applies to the website https://mantrikagames.com and all digital board game releases published by Mantrika Games ("we", "us", or "our"), including our Android releases distributed through the Google Play Store:

2. Information Collection & Use

We do not collect or require personal identification information (such as your legal name, physical address, financial accounts, or phone number) to play our games. The vast majority of our games can be enjoyed completely anonymously.

Technical & Telemetry Data We May Collect:

  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, GPU renderer details (for 3D WebGL optimization), and system language.
  • Gameplay Telemetry: Non-personal aggregated metrics (such as match completion rates, game difficulty settings, move counts, and AI victory rates) to balance game mechanics.
  • Crash & Exception Logs: Stack traces and error timestamps collected automatically via system logging when a game engine encounters an unhandled runtime error.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To facilitate application delivery, crash diagnostic monitoring, and testing distribution, we work with trusted infrastructure providers that may process telemetry according to their privacy guidelines:

  • Google Play Services: Used for secure APK/AAB distribution, license verification, and internal beta testing management. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts our static web assets, HTML5 scripts, and WebGL game engines with SSL encryption and DDoS protection. (GitHub Privacy Statement)

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses strictly essential browser storage (such as localStorage) exclusively for client-side user preferences:

  • Remembering your Dark/Light theme mode preference.
  • Remembering your Cookie banner dismissal status.
  • Remembering local game progress, sound volume toggles, and single-player match states.

We do not utilize cross-site tracking cookies or marketing pixels.

5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

We retain diagnostic crash logs and beta feedback only for as long as necessary to identify bugs and enhance stability. You have the right to request full deletion of any associated tester information or email records.

To submit a formal deletion request, visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Request page or email us directly at mantrikagames@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Our board games and educational strategic puzzles are suitable for players of all ages, including families and children. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
