Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 12, 2026

Mantrika Games ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website https://mantrikagames.com and the "Board of Mills" mobile application (collectively, the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of any information collected when you use our Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy, terms used in this Privacy Policy have the same meanings as in our Terms of Service.

1. Information Collection & Use

We do not collect or request any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) during gameplay. However, we may collect non-personal data to monitor app health, improve gameplay experience, and understand user behavior.

2. Data We May Collect

  • Device Information: We may collect info regarding your mobile device or browser, including the device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Statistics: We track basic interactions within the app (such as match completion, selected game types, and game settings) to help us refine and improve our game rules and design.
  • Log and Crash Data: In the event of a runtime crash or error, we automatically collect crash logs containing technical stack traces to help us fix bugs quickly.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or track application telemetry. Links to the privacy policies of these providers can be found below:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
  • Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config)

4. Cookies & Web Storage

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser. Our website uses essential cookies and local storage to remember your website preference settings, such as your theme choice (Light/Dark mode) and cookie consent status. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though doing so might affect some website functionalities.

5. Data Security

The security of your data is of paramount importance to us. We employ standard, industry-accepted physical, technical, and administrative protections to secure your data. However, please remember that no transmission method over the internet or database storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is designed for general audiences and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary corrective action.

7. Account and Data Deletion

If you have created an account within our mobile application, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data. To request account and data deletion, please contact us at contact@mantrikagames.com. Upon receiving your request, we will delete your login credentials and gameplay profiles from our database within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at contact@mantrikagames.com.
