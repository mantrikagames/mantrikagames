The Seesaw Trap (Double Mill)

The ultimate offensive formation in any Mill game is moving a single piece back and forth between two incomplete mills, capturing an opposing piece on every single turn.

✦

Corner vs Cross Intersections

Middle-line intersections command 4 connected pathways, whereas corners command only 2. In Twelve Men's Morris and Morabaraba, corner diagonals elevate corners into lethal hubs.

✦

The Late-Game Flying Phase

When reduced to 3 pieces, a player gains "flying" privileges—the ability to hop to any open intersection on the entire board, turning imminent defeat into sudden tactical comebacks.
