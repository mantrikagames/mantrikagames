About Mantrika Games

Mantrika Games bridges centuries of history with modern digital craftsmanship. We preserve cultural heritage by bringing ancient strategy board games from different cultures into the digital world, combining elegant design, adaptive artificial intelligence, and seamless multiplayer.

Alongside our digital releases, we honor the tactile history of tabletop gaming. Every title is accompanied by a premium physical edition, handcrafted from sustainably sourced wood and local materials to create heirloom-quality pieces that can be passed down for generations.

"Strategy is universal, but expression is cultural. We build bridges between centuries-old board carvings and responsive mobile screens."
Mantrika design philosophy
