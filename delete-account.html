Account & Data Deletion Request

Manage your privacy and request complete deletion of your profile data across Mantrika Games applications.

Supported Applications

This data deletion policy applies to all official Mantrika Games titles:

Board of Mills Gomoku World RealmForge Reversi World

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon submitting an account deletion request, the following data associated with your user ID will be permanently removed from our databases within 30 days:

  • Authentication Credentials: Your registered email address, authentication tokens, and user profile ID.
  • Player Profile: Display name, selected avatar choices, and account registration timestamp.
  • Gameplay Statistics & Leaderboards: Strategic rating points, win/loss history, total games played, and active leaderboard standings.
  • In-App Wallet Data: Coin balances and item unlock logs.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to mantrikagames@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account.
  2. Set the subject line to: Account Deletion Request - [App Name]
  3. Include your Display Name and/or User ID (found in the app settings) in the body of the email.

Retention Period & Confirmation

Account deletion requests are processed manually within 30 days. Once your data has been permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, you will receive a confirmation email.
