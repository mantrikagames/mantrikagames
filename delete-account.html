User Sovereignty & Compliance

Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy standards, players have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account records, beta test logs, and telemetry.

How to Request Data Deletion

Because Mantrika Games prioritizes local client-side play and does not require complex passwords or forced social logins, most player interactions are tied strictly to an anonymous install ID or your Google Play testing email.

1

Submit Your Deletion Request

Send an email from your testing account or registered email to mantrikagames@gmail.com with the subject "Account and Data Deletion Request".

2

Specify Games & Identifiers

Please indicate which game(s) you participated in (e.g., Board of Mills, Gomoku World) and your Google Play tester email address.

3

Verification & Permanent Purge

Our team will acknowledge your request within 48 hours and permanently delete all associated tester records, feedback submissions, and diagnostic logs within 30 days.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Google Play Tester Roster: Your email is permanently removed from the internal/closed testing track.
  • Crash & Telemetry Logs: All diagnostic logs matching your device session IDs are irrevocably purged.
  • Correspondence & Feedback: Any support communications or bug reports sent via email are deleted upon request.

What Data is Retained?

Because we do not maintain central cloud databases storing player credit cards, passwords, or personal profiles, no secondary commercial records are retained once the testing logs are purged.
